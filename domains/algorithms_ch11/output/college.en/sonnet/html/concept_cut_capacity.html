Cut Capacity

Definition. Let $G = (V, E)$ be a directed graph with a distinguished source $s \in V$ and sink $t \in V$. Each edge $(u, v) \in E$ carries a non-negative capacity $c(u, v) \geq 0$. An $s$-$t$ cut is a partition $(S, T)$ of $V$ such that $s \in S$ and $t \in T$. The cut capacity is the total capacity of edges crossing from $S$ to $T$:

$$\text{cap}(S, T) = \sum_{\substack{u \in S,\, v \in T \\ (u,v) \in E}} c(u, v).$$

Note carefully that only forward-crossing edges—those directed from $S$ into $T$—contribute to $\text{cap}(S, T)$. Edges directed from $T$ back into $S$ are ignored in the capacity sum, though they matter when computing the net flow across the cut.

Worked Example. Consider the graph with $V = \{s, a, b, t\}$ and edges $c(s,a)=3$, $c(s,b)=2$, $c(a,t)=2$, $c(b,t)=3$, $c(a,b)=1$. Take the cut $S = \{s, a\}$, $T = \{b, t\}$. The forward-crossing edges are $(s,b)$ and $(a,t)$ and $(a,b)$, giving

$$\text{cap}(S,T) = c(s,b) + c(a,t) + c(a,b) = 2 + 2 + 1 = 5.$$

The cut $S' = \{s\}$, $T' = \{a,b,t\}$ yields $\text{cap}(S',T') = c(s,a)+c(s,b) = 3+2 = 5$ as well. The minimum cut in this network has capacity $4$, achieved by $S^ = \{s,a,b\}$, $T^ = \{t\}$ with forward edges $(a,t)$ and $(b,t)$.

Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem (Ford–Fulkerson, 1956). For any $s$-$t$ network, the maximum value of any feasible $s$-$t$ flow equals the minimum capacity over all $s$-$t$ cuts:

$$\max_f |f| = \min_{(S,T)} \text{cap}(S, T).$$

The proof proceeds by showing that when no augmenting path remains in the residual graph, the reachable set from $s$ in the residual graph defines a cut whose capacity exactly equals the current flow value, establishing both bounds simultaneously.

import sympy as sp
from sympy.combinatorics.named_groups import SymmetricGroup

# Represent capacities as a dictionary and compute cut capacity
cap = {('s','a'): 3, ('s','b'): 2, ('a','t'): 2, ('b','t'): 3, ('a','b'): 1}

def cut_capacity(cap, S, T):
    return sum(c for (u, v), c in cap.items() if u in S and v in T)

S = {'s', 'a'}
T = {'b', 't'}
print(cut_capacity(cap, S, T))  # Expected: 5
