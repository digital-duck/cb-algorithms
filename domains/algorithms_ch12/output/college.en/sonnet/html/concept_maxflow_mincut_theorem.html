Maxflow Mincut Theorem

Definition. Let $G = (V, E)$ be a directed graph with a source $s \in V$, a sink $t \in V$, and a capacity function $c : E \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$. A flow is a function $f : E \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ satisfying (i) $0 \leq f(u,v) \leq c(u,v)$ for every edge $(u,v)$, and (ii) flow conservation: $\sum_{u} f(u,v) = \sum_{w} f(v,w)$ for every internal node $v \neq s,t$. The value of the flow is $|f| = \sum_{v} f(s,v) - \sum_{v} f(v,s)$. An $s$-$t$ cut is a partition $(S, T)$ of $V$ with $s \in S$ and $t \in T$; its capacity is $c(S,T) = \sum_{u \in S,\, v \in T} c(u,v)$.

Worked Example. Consider the graph with edges and capacities: $c(s,a)=3$, $c(s,b)=2$, $c(a,t)=2$, $c(b,t)=3$, $c(a,b)=1$. One feasible flow assigns $f(s,a)=2$, $f(s,b)=2$, $f(a,t)=2$, $f(b,t)=2$, giving $|f|=4$. The cut $S=\{s,a\}$, $T=\{b,t\}$ has capacity $c(s,b)+c(a,t)+c(a,b)=2+2+1=5$, while the cut $S=\{s\}$, $T=\{a,b,t\}$ has capacity $c(s,a)+c(s,b)=5$. The tighter cut $S=\{s,a,b\}$ yields capacity $c(a,t)+c(b,t)=2+3=5$. A careful search reveals that the maximum flow is indeed $4$, achieved by routing one additional unit through $s \to a \to b \to t$.

Key Theorem (Max-Flow Min-Cut, Ford–Fulkerson 1956). For any network $(G, s, t, c)$, the maximum value of an $s$-$t$ flow equals the minimum capacity of an $s$-$t$ cut:

$$\max_{f} |f| \;=\; \min_{(S,T)} c(S,T).$$

Proof sketch. The weak duality inequality $|f| \leq c(S,T)$ follows immediately from flow conservation and capacity constraints. For equality, run the Ford–Fulkerson algorithm to obtain a flow $f^$ with no augmenting path in the residual graph $G_{f^}$. Define $S^$ as the set of nodes reachable from $s$ in $G_{f^}$ and $T^ = V \setminus S^$. Every forward edge $(u,v)$ with $u \in S^, v \in T^$ must be saturated ($f(u,v)=c(u,v)$), otherwise $v$ would be reachable. Hence $|f^| = c(S^,T^*)$, establishing the bound is tight. $\blacksquare$

from sympy import symbols, Rational

# Represent capacities as a dict and verify weak duality
cap = {('s','a'):3, ('s','b'):2, ('a','t'):2, ('b','t'):3, ('a','b'):1}
flow = {('s','a'):2, ('s','b'):2, ('a','t'):2, ('b','t'):2, ('a','b'):0}

flow_value = flow[('s','a')] + flow[('s','b')]
cut_S = {'s', 'a', 'b'}
cut_capacity = sum(v for (u,w),v in cap.items() if u in cut_S and w not in cut_S)

assert flow_value <= cut_capacity, "Weak duality violated"
print(f"Flow value: {flow_value}, Min-cut capacity: {cut_capacity}")
