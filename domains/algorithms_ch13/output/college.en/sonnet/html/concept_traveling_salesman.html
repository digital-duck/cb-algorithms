Traveling Salesman

Definition. Let $G = (V, E, w)$ be a complete weighted undirected graph, where $V = \{v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_n\}$ is a set of $n$ cities, $E$ contains all $\binom{n}{2}$ edges, and $w : E \to \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ assigns a positive cost to each edge. A Hamiltonian cycle is a closed walk that visits every vertex exactly once. The Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) asks for a Hamiltonian cycle of minimum total weight:

$$\text{minimize} \quad \sum_{i=1}^{n} w(v_{\sigma(i)}, v_{\sigma(i+1)}),$$

where $\sigma$ is a permutation of $\{1, \ldots, n\}$ and indices are taken modulo $n$. TSP belongs to the complexity class $\mathbf{NP}$-hard; no polynomial-time exact algorithm is known unless $\mathbf{P} = \mathbf{NP}$.

Worked Example. Consider four cities with the following symmetric distance matrix:

$$D = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 10 & 15 & 20 \\ 10 & 0 & 35 & 25 \\ 15 & 35 & 0 & 30 \\ 20 & 25 & 30 & 0 \end{pmatrix}.$$

There are $(4-1)!/2 = 3$ distinct tours. Enumerating them:

| Tour | Total Cost | |------|------------| | $v_1 \to v_2 \to v_3 \to v_4 \to v_1$ | $10+35+30+20 = 95$ | | $v_1 \to v_2 \to v_4 \to v_3 \to v_1$ | $10+25+30+15 = 80$ | | $v_1 \to v_3 \to v_2 \to v_4 \to v_1$ | $15+35+25+20 = 95$ |

The optimal tour is $v_1 \to v_2 \to v_4 \to v_3 \to v_1$ with cost $80$.

Key Theorem (Nearest-Neighbor Approximation Bound). For metric instances of TSP — where edge weights satisfy the triangle inequality $w(u,v) \leq w(u,x) + w(x,v)$ — the Christofides–Serdyukov algorithm guarantees a tour of cost at most $\tfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{OPT}$, where $\mathrm{OPT}$ denotes the optimal tour length. No polynomial-time approximation scheme with ratio better than $\tfrac{123}{122}$ exists unless $\mathbf{P} = \mathbf{NP}$ (Karpinski et al., 2015).

Lab Cell.

from itertools import permutations
import sympy as sp

D = [[0,10,15,20],[10,0,35,25],[15,35,0,30],[20,25,30,0]]
n = len(D)

def tour_cost(perm):
    return sum(D[perm[i]][perm[(i+1) % n]] for i in range(n))

cities = list(range(n))
best_cost = sp.oo
best_tour = None

for perm in permutations(cities[1:]):
    tour = (cities[0],) + perm
    cost = tour_cost(tour)
    if cost < best_cost:
        best_cost, best_tour = cost, tour

print("Optimal tour:", best_tour, "| Cost:", best_cost)
