Conjunctive Normal Form

Definition. A propositional formula is in conjunctive normal form (CNF) if it is written as a conjunction of one or more clauses, where each clause is a disjunction of one or more literals. A literal is either a propositional variable $p$ or its negation $\neg p$. Formally, a CNF formula has the shape

$$\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m} \bigvee_{j=1}^{n_i} \ell_{i,j}$$

where each $\ell_{i,j}$ is a literal. Every propositional formula is logically equivalent to some CNF formula; this normal form is the standard input language for SAT solvers.

Worked Example. Convert $\varphi = (p \Rightarrow q) \land (\neg q \lor r)$ to CNF.

Step 1 — Eliminate implications. Replace $p \Rightarrow q$ with $\neg p \lor q$:

$$\varphi \equiv (\neg p \lor q) \land (\neg q \lor r).$$

Step 2 — Push negations inward (De Morgan). No double negations or complex negations remain, so the formula is already in CNF. The two clauses are $C_1 = \{\neg p, q\}$ and $C_2 = \{\neg q, r\}$.

A more instructive conversion arises with $\psi = \neg(p \land \neg q)$. Applying De Morgan: $\neg p \lor q$, a single-clause CNF.

Key Theorem (Tseitin, 1968). Every propositional formula $\varphi$ with $n$ subformulas can be converted to an equisatisfiable CNF formula of size $O(n)$ by introducing at most $n$ fresh auxiliary variables — one per connective. The resulting CNF is not logically equivalent to $\varphi$ (it lives in an extended variable space), but it is satisfiable if and only if $\varphi$ is satisfiable. This linear-size guarantee makes Tseitin encoding the standard pre-processing step for industrial SAT solvers, avoiding the exponential blowup that naive distribution of $\lor$ over $\land$ can cause.

Lab Cell.

from sympy import symbols, Implies, to_cnf

p, q, r = symbols('p q r')

phi = Implies(p, q) & (~q | r)
print("CNF:", to_cnf(phi))          # already CNF

psi = ~(p & ~q)
print("CNF:", to_cnf(psi))          # De Morgan → ¬p ∨ q

# Tseitin-style: introduce auxiliary variable
a = symbols('a')
tseitin_clause = (a | ~p) & (~a | p) & (a | ~q)
print("Tseitin clauses:", to_cnf(tseitin_clause))
