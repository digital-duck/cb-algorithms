Safe Edge

Definition. Let $G = (V, E, w)$ be a connected, weighted, undirected graph with distinct edge weights, and let $A \subseteq E$ be a subset of edges that is contained in some minimum spanning tree (MST) of $G$. An edge $(u, v) \in E \setminus A$ is called a safe edge for $A$ if $A \cup \{(u,v)\}$ is still contained in some MST of $G$. The notion formalises the invariant maintained by greedy MST algorithms: at every step, we may extend $A$ by a safe edge without jeopardising optimality.

To state the characterisation precisely, recall two definitions. A cut of $G$ is a bipartition $(S,\, V \setminus S)$ of the vertex set. An edge crosses the cut if exactly one of its endpoints lies in $S$. A cut respects $A$ if no edge in $A$ crosses it. Among all edges crossing a given cut, a light edge is one of minimum weight.

Worked Example. Consider the graph with vertices $\{a, b, c, d\}$ and edges $ab{=}1,\, ac{=}4,\, bc{=}2,\, bd{=}5,\, cd{=}3$. Suppose $A = \{ab\}$, already part of the eventual MST. Take the cut $S = \{a, b\}$, $V \setminus S = \{c, d\}$. This cut respects $A$ (neither endpoint of $ab$ lies on opposite sides). The edges crossing the cut are $ac{=}4$, $bc{=}2$, $bd{=}5$, $cd{=}3$. The light edge is $bc$ with weight $2$. By the theorem below, $bc$ is safe for $A$, so we may set $A \leftarrow \{ab, bc\}$ and remain on the optimal path.

Theorem (Safe-Edge / Cut Property). Let $A$ be a subset of some MST. If $(S, V \setminus S)$ is any cut that respects $A$, and $(u, v)$ is a light edge crossing that cut, then $(u, v)$ is safe for $A$.

Proof sketch. Let $T$ be an MST containing $A$. If $(u,v) \in T$ we are done. Otherwise, adding $(u,v)$ to $T$ creates a unique cycle $C$. Since $T$ is a spanning tree, $C$ must contain another edge $(x,y)$ that also crosses $(S, V\setminus S)$. Because $(u,v)$ is a light edge, $w(u,v) \leq w(x,y)$. Replacing $(x,y)$ with $(u,v)$ yields a spanning tree $T'$ with $w(T') \leq w(T)$, so $T'$ is also an MST, and $A \cup \{(u,v)\} \subseteq T'$. $\blacksquare$

Lab Cell (SymPy).

import sympy as sp
from sympy.combinatorics.named_groups import *

# Represent a small weighted graph and verify the safe-edge criterion
edges = {('a','b'):1, ('a','c'):4, ('b','c'):2, ('b','d'):5, ('c','d'):3}
A = {('a','b')}
S = {'a','b'}

crossing = {e: w for e, w in edges.items()
            if (e[0] in S) != (e[1] in S)}
respects  = all(e not in crossing for e in A)
light     = min(crossing, key=crossing.get)

print("Cut respects A:", respects)
print("Light (safe) edge:", light, "weight:", crossing[light])
